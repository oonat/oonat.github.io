I am a PhD student at the [School of Informatics, University of Edinburgh](https://www.ed.ac.uk/informatics) and currently a research intern at [Five AI](https://www.five.ai/) in Cambridge, working on **vision–language–action models for autonomous driving**.

My research interests lie in **interpretability and representation analysis for multimodal models**: what these models actually encode, how they arrive at a prediction, and when they should not be trusted. I have also worked on uncertainty and out-of-distribution detection in image classification.

Before Edinburgh I was an Applied Scientist at RadiusAI (2022–2025), where I built VLM-based visual search and multi-camera systems for retail environments. I completed my BSc and MSc in Computer Engineering at [Middle East Technical University (METU)](https://www.metu.edu.tr/), where my MSc thesis focused on interpretable zero-shot classification using concept bottleneck models.

I am always happy to talk about interpretability, multimodal representations, and embodied vision–language models.
